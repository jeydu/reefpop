Privacy Policy

Reef Pop · Last updated 27 November 2026

Reef Pop is a puzzle game published by JADULLA JADULLA AHMED. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

We don't collect your data

Reef Pop does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores) and your sound and music settings are saved only on your phone, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Children

Reef Pop is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
