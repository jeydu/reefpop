Reef Pop

An underwater match-3 puzzle game for iPhone

Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for the game? Open a request on our support page and we'll get back to you.

How to play

  • Swipe a piece onto its neighbour, or tap two neighbours, to swap them. Line up 3 or more of the same kind to clear them.
  • Match 4 in a line for a Wave Blaster, an L or T shape for a Bubble Bomb, and 5 in a line for a Whirlpool.
  • Sand clears when you match on top of it. Packed sand takes two matches.
  • Reach each level's goal before you run out of moves to unlock the next one. 315 levels across nine underwater trenches.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

No sound? Check that your iPhone isn't on silent, and that sound and music are switched on in the pause menu.
